How to cook
  • Line up four of a flavor — spices and ingredients both count — and they dissolve.
  • Season every ingredient to serve the dish. Let the pot fill to the rim and it boils over.
  • Drag sideways to move. Tap the left or right half to turn. Drag down to drop faster, flick down to throw.
  • Keys: ← → move, ↓ drop, ↑ / X and Z turn, Space throws, P pauses.
  • Chains fill your mill. A full mill puts a peppercorn in your next pinch — it matches anything.
